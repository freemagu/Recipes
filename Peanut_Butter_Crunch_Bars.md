# Peanut Butter Crunch Bars

> No-bake peanut butter and puffed rice bars with sugar-free maple-style syrup and 85% dark chocolate.

**Makes:** 10 bars  
**Tin:** Approximately 23 × 10 cm loaf tin  
**Chilling time:** 1-3 hours, until firm  
**Measurements:** US cups, with approximate gram equivalents in brackets.

The frontmatter carbs_g value uses the same UK/EU carbohydrate convention shown in the table, excluding fibre. Exact values depend on ingredient labels. Sugar-free syrup does not make the finished bars sugar-free: chocolate and peanuts still contribute sugar.

## Ingredients

### Peanut butter layer

- [ ] ¾ cup smooth, unsweetened peanut butter [190 g]
- [ ] ⅓ cup sugar-free maple-style syrup [approximately 80 g, depending on brand]
- [ ] ⅓ cup melted coconut oil [73 g]
- [ ] 2 cups puffed rice [approximately 28 g]

### Chocolate layer

- [ ] Approximately 1 cup finely chopped 85% dark chocolate [170 g; use the weight for accuracy]
- [ ] 1 tablespoon coconut oil [14 g]
- [ ] 1 cup puffed rice [approximately 14 g]

## Method

- [ ] 1 - Line the loaf tin with baking paper.
- [ ] 2 - Mix the peanut butter, sugar-free syrup and 73 g melted coconut oil until combined.
- [ ] 3 - Stir in the two cups of puffed rice. Press firmly into the lined tin.
- [ ] 4 - Gently melt the chocolate with the tablespoon of coconut oil, then stir in the remaining cup of puffed rice.
- [ ] 5 - Spread the chocolate mixture over the peanut butter layer.
- [ ] 6 - Refrigerate for 1-3 hours, until firm, then cut into 10 bars.

## Notes

- Keep refrigerated in an airtight container.
- Puffed rice weights vary considerably by brand. Use the cup measurements unless your packet gives a weight per cup; the estimates above assume 14 g per cup.
- Measure sugar-free syrup by volume: its weight depends on the brand and will differ from regular maple syrup.
- A watery sugar-free syrup may soften the cereal more than maple syrup. Prefer a thick maple-style syrup.
- Adapted from [Sam Does Her Best: Peanut Butter Crunch Bars](https://samdoesherbest.com/peanut-butter-crunch-bars/), replacing maple syrup with sugar-free maple-style syrup and chocolate chips with 85% dark chocolate.
