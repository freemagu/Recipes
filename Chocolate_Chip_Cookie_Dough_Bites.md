# Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bites

**Makes:** 10-12 bites

## Ingredients

- 80g Lakanto Monkfruit Sweetened Chocolate Chip Cookie Mix
- 50g Philadelphia Balance cream cheese
- 20g almond butter
- 15g butter, softened
- Pinch of salt, optional

## Method

1. For food safety, spread the dry cookie mixture on a baking tray and heat-treat it at 160°C for 8-10 minutes, stirring once halfway through.
2. Allow the cookie mixture to cool completely before continuing.
3. Add the cooled cookie mixture, cream cheese, almond butter, softened butter, and optional salt to a bowl.
4. Mix thoroughly until a smooth, thick cookie dough forms.
5. If the mixture is too sticky, add a little more cookie mixture. If it is too dry, add a teaspoon of cream cheese.
6. Divide and roll into 10-12 bite-sized balls.
7. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before eating.

## Storage

- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.
- For a firmer texture, keep chilled until serving.
- The bites can also be frozen and allowed to soften for a few minutes before eating.
