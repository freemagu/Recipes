# Garlic Butter Steak Bites & Creamy Mash

**Makes:** 4 meals

## Ingredients

### Garlic Butter Steak Bites

- 900g lean steak, diced (flank steak with the fat trimmed works well)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- Cooking spray
- 15-20g butter
- 15g minced garlic
- Fresh parsley, chopped

### Creamy Garlic Mash

- 900g potatoes, peeled and chopped
- Salt, for boiling
- 15g butter
- 80g light cream cheese
- 120ml low-fat milk, or 0% fat milk if preferred
- 30g roasted garlic paste
- 1 tsp onion powder
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Garnish

- Fresh chopped parsley
- Melted butter, optional

## Method

1. Dice the steak into bite-sized cubes and season with the garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Toss well until evenly coated.
2. Heat a large non-stick pan over high heat and lightly coat with cooking spray. Add the steak and cook until browned and crisp on all sides while remaining tender inside.
3. Reduce the heat slightly, then add the butter, minced garlic, and chopped parsley. Toss the steak until evenly coated in the garlic butter, then remove from the heat.
4. Meanwhile, boil the chopped potatoes in salted water until fork tender, then drain well.
5. Mash the potatoes until smooth, then stir in the butter, cream cheese, milk, roasted garlic paste, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Mix until rich, creamy, and smooth.
6. Divide the creamy mash evenly between four meal-prep containers. Top with the garlic butter steak bites and garnish with chopped parsley. Drizzle with a little melted butter if desired.

## Storage & Heating

- Store in the fridge for up to 5 days.
- To reheat, add the contents of the meal-prep container to a pan over medium heat.
- Heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until warmed through.
