# Premium Salted Pistachio Gelato

> Rich, intensely nutty Ninja Creami pistachio gelato with a subtle salted finish.

## Ingredients

### Gelato base

- [ ] 350 ml whole milk
- [ ] 100 ml double cream, approximately 35% fat
- [ ] 130 g 100% pistachio paste
- [ ] 55 g caster sugar
- [ ] 20 g full-cream milk powder
- [ ] 1 g xanthan gum, approximately ⅓ tsp
- [ ] 1.5 g fine sea salt, approximately ¼ tsp
- [ ] 4 drops almond extract
- [ ] 3 drops green food colouring

### Optional mix-in

- [ ] 25 g roasted pistachios, cooled and roughly chopped

## Method

- [ ] 1 - Mix the caster sugar, full-cream milk powder and xanthan gum thoroughly in a bowl. Mixing the xanthan into the dry ingredients first prevents clumping.
- [ ] 2 - Add the whole milk gradually while whisking until the dry ingredients are fully dispersed.
- [ ] 3 - Add the double cream, pistachio paste, sea salt, almond extract and green food colouring.
- [ ] 4 - Blend until completely smooth. Avoid excessive blending once smooth, as unnecessary air makes the mixture foamier.
- [ ] 5 - Pour into a 700 ml Ninja Creami Deluxe tub, keeping the mixture below the MAX FILL line. Fit the storage lid.
- [ ] 6 - Refrigerate for 8-12 hours. This ages the base, fully hydrates the milk powder and xanthan gum, and rounds out the pistachio flavour.
- [ ] 7 - Stir gently, tap the tub on the worktop to release trapped bubbles, then freeze level for 24-36 hours.
- [ ] 8 - Process once using the Ice Cream programme. Do not Re-Spin when it is already smooth and soft.
- [ ] 9 - For added texture, make a well in the centre, add 25 g chopped roasted pistachios and run the Mix-In programme.
- [ ] 10 - Serve immediately, or return the processed tub to the freezer for 10-15 minutes for a firmer scoop.

## Notes

- This is an egg-free gelato base. No custard cooking or vanilla is required, which keeps the pistachio flavour clean and prominent.
- The tested 130 g pistachio-paste level gives a strong, premium flavour and is deliberately retained.
- The original version used 130 ml cream and softened very quickly. Reducing the cream to 100 ml and using approximately 1 g xanthan gum should improve shape retention while preserving richness.
- Because the recipe contains substantial sugar, dairy fat and pistachio oil, it will naturally soften faster than lower-fat ice cream. Keep bowls chilled and serve promptly.
- If the first spin is powdery rather than creamy, add 1 tbsp whole milk and Re-Spin once. Do not add liquid automatically.
- Estimated yield: one 700 ml Ninja Creami Deluxe tub.
- Nutrition is approximate and includes the optional 25 g pistachio mix-in. Exact values depend on the milk, cream, milk powder and pistachio paste used.
