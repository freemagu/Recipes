# Flourless Lemon Greek Yoghurt Cake

A moist, lemony cake that tastes somewhere between lemon drizzle cake and lemon cheesecake when served chilled.

## Shopping List

- 180 g full-fat Greek yoghurt
- 180 g fat-free Greek yoghurt
- 3 large eggs
- 200 g ground almonds or almond flour
- 65-70 g Whole Earth zero-calorie sweetener
- 2 medium lemons
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Optional Lemon Glaze
- 30 g Whole Earth sweetener, powdered
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- Extra lemon zest

## Ingredients

- 180 g full-fat Greek yoghurt
- 180 g fat-free Greek yoghurt
- 3 large eggs
- 200 g ground almonds or almond flour
- 65-70 g Whole Earth zero-calorie sweetener
- Zest of 2 medium lemons
- 60 ml fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

### Optional Lemon Glaze
- 30 g Whole Earth sweetener, powdered
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- Extra lemon zest

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 175°C.
2. Grease and line an 8-inch (20 cm) round cake tin.
3. Whisk together the yoghurts, eggs, sweetener, lemon juice, lemon zest and vanilla until smooth.
4. Stir in the ground almonds, baking powder and salt until fully combined.
5. Pour into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
6. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until the centre is set and a skewer comes out clean.
7. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes and then transfer to a wire rack.
8. For the optional glaze, whisk together the powdered sweetener and lemon juice and drizzle lightly over the cooled cake. Finish with extra lemon zest.
9. Chill overnight for the best flavour and texture.
