# Buffalo Chicken Hot Pockets

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 400g all-purpose flour
- 400g fat-free Greek yogurt

### Buffalo Chicken Filling
- 500g chicken breast, diced
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Smoked paprika
- Cayenne pepper
- Salt
- Black pepper
- 30g butter
- 130ml hot sauce
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- 20g stevia
- 25g honey
- 10g cornstarch
- 160g low-fat mozzarella, grated
- Cooking spray

## Method
1. Combine the flour and Greek yogurt into a smooth dough. Knead for 3 to 5 minutes. Divide into 8 portions.
2. Season the chicken with the spices.
3. Spray a pan with cooking spray and cook the chicken until done.
4. Melt the butter, then stir in the hot sauce, vinegar, stevia and honey. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Mix the cornstarch with cold water and stir into the sauce until thickened.
6. Coat the chicken in the sauce and allow to cool slightly.
7. Roll each dough ball into an 18 to 20cm circle.
8. Fill each with one-eighth of the chicken and about 20g mozzarella.
9. Fold over, seal and crimp the edges.
10. Pan cook over medium-low heat with a lid for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.

## Storage
- Refrigerate for up to 5 days.
- Freeze individually for longer storage.
- Reheat in the microwave, pan or air fryer.
